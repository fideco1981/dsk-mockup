# Drammen Slalåmklubb site

Glossary for the club website. Decisions live in `docs/adr/`.

- **Event** (Arrangement, Sanity type `event`): a dated club happening shown on the landing page. Has a date and an optional end date for multi-day events (e.g. BAMA, Friday–Sunday). Only upcoming ones are shown (until their last day), nearest first, at most three.
- **Topbar message** (Sanity type `topbarMessage`): the site-wide announcement line at the top of every page. A singleton holding an ordered list of messages (text and optional link) and one show/hide toggle. Several messages rotate every 5 s, with a pause button.
- **Sponsor** (Sanity type `sponsor`): a club sponsor shown as a logo in the strip at the top of every page's footer, sorted by its order field. One of two Sanity types with an image (ADR 0002, 0003). It has no Fallback content: the strip is hidden when Sanity fails.
- **Training group** (Treningsgruppe, Sanity type `trainingGroup`): a training group on `treningsopplegg.html` (Grønn, Blå, Rød, Sort, Fun), sorted by its order field. Has a colour, optional age, description, place, price and weekly sessions (day, from, to), shown one per line Monday→Sunday. Its anchor is a slug of the title (`#gronn`). The quick-nav cards, the sections and the landing page's "Våre Treningsgrupper" cards all come from the same groups. Sanity failing or returning no groups shows the Fallback content.
- **Ski school** (Skiskole, Sanity type `skiSchool`): a singleton holding the text on `skiskole.html` shared by every Course: intro, age, what the course fee includes (a list), equipment, meetup and reservation. If Sanity fails or it doesn't exist yet, the page shows its Fallback content.
- **Course** (Kurs, Sanity type `course`): a dated ski-school course on `skiskole.html`, entered by hand to mirror Spond. Has a title, start and end date, an optional signup deadline (shown as text only), weekly sessions (the same as a Training group's), skipWeeks (ISO weeks without a session, e.g. winter break), price, a Spond link and a note. Courses are shown until their end date, by start date. With none left, the page says so and links to Spond.
- **Lift hours** (Heistider, Sanity type `liftHours`): a singleton holding the weekly lift hours (at most one from–to per day; a day without one is closed) and a note. The lift is open to everyone from its opening time. There are no seasonal periods or date exceptions; the note covers them. Shown in compact form on `om-oss.html` and `kontakt.html` ("Man–Tor 18:00–20:00 · Fre stengt · Lør–Søn 11:00–15:00") and as per-day lift labels on the landing page. If Sanity fails or it doesn't exist yet, the pages show their Fallback content.
- **Training plan** (Treningsplan, no Sanity type): the landing page's weekly view (`#schedule`), built from Training group sessions, the Courses running in the current week (Monday–Sunday, minus their skipWeeks) and Lift hours; it has no data of its own. Each day lists its sessions by start time, groups in their colour and Courses in one shared ski-school colour, each labelled "Skiskole" rather than its title (legend entry "Skiskole" only when a Course is shown). That colour is fixed in `styles.css` (`.group-skiskole`) and is not one of the Training group colours listed under Chip. When a Course starts before the lift opens and runs up to opening, the lift bar is extended back with a "Kun skiskole" piece. Likewise, a session running past lift closing gets a "Kun <group>" piece at the end, in its colour. Sanity failing or returning no groups shows the Fallback: the five group names, the lift hours and a pointer to `treningsopplegg.html`, with no session times.
- **Board member** (Styremedlem, an entry in the Sanity singleton `board`): a person on `styret.html` with a position (Styreleder, Nestleder, Sportslig leder, Styremedlem) or as a deputy (Vara), in a set order. The position list lives in `studio/schemaTypes/board.js`; `site.js` matches "Vara" by name to put deputies in their own grid, so renaming or adding positions means updating both together (as with Chip). A separate list from Contact persons; a person in both is entered twice. May carry a phone and email, shown only with Consent. The board's term (elected at which årsmøte, sitting until which) is one line of text alongside the list. With Sanity failing, Styret is hidden (no Fallback content).
- **Contact person** (Kontaktperson): a person on `kontakt.html` with a free-text role (e.g. "Foreldrekontakt · U12–U14"), in a set order, kept in Club info. Not linked to Board member. May carry a phone and email, shown only with Consent.
- **Trainer** (Trener, an entry in the Sanity singleton `trainers`): an adult (18+) trainer or ski-school instructor on `trenere.html`, in a set order. Has a name, an optional role (e.g. "Hovedtrener"), a presentation (at most 400 characters) and merits (coaching qualifications included). Tagged with the Training groups and/or Ski school (Skiskole) they're responsible for, each tag linking to its page. May carry a photo, shown only with its own photo Consent (ADR 0005). A separate list from Board member; a person in both is entered twice, each with its own Consent. May carry a phone and email, shown only with Consent; without them the card shows no "kontakt via" line. With Sanity failing or no trainers, the trainer grid is hidden (no Fallback content).
- **Club info** (Klubbinfo, Sanity type `clubInfo`): a singleton holding the club's addresses, org number, social links (one optional URL each for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok; an empty one hides that button), Spond signup link, the membership call-to-action text and membership points, and the Contact persons, shown on `kontakt.html`, in the footer and on the landing page.
- **Featured post** (Utvalgt innlegg, Sanity type `featuredPost`): a hand-picked post shown as a square tile in the landing page's socials section: image (cropped in Studio, or fetched from a TikTok link), alt text, a link to anywhere (Instagram, TikTok, …) and an order. The first six by order are shown. Not a feed: it changes only when an editor changes it. No Fallback content: the tiles are hidden when Sanity fails, and the follow buttons (from Club info) stay.
- **Consent** (Samtykke): a per-person checkbox and date on a Board member, Contact person or Trainer, recording that they agreed to have their phone and email published. Studio won't publish phone or email without it, because the dataset is public (ADR 0004). A Trainer's photo has its own Consent (checkbox and date), separate from phone and email (ADR 0005). The evidence is kept by the club outside the site.
- **Studio**: the Sanity editing app, sourced from `studio/` and hosted on `*.sanity.studio`. Where editors change Events, the Topbar message, Sponsors, Training groups, the Ski school, Courses, Lift hours, the Board (Styret), the Trainers (Trenere), Club info and Featured posts.
- **Fallback content**: the hand-written HTML shown without JS or when Sanity fails. A snapshot that may drift; Sanity wins whenever it answers. Only content a visitor acts on (contact details, opening hours, schedules, courses) gets Fallback content; decorative or informational sections (e.g. Styret, stats) are hidden when Sanity fails.
- **Chip**: a fixed-vocabulary event tag with a colour (Renn, Gratis, Årsmøte, Medlemmer). Adding one means updating the Studio schema and the colour map in `site.js` together. Training groups have their own colour list (Grønn, Blå, Rød, Sort, Orange), kept the same way in the schema, `site.js` and `styles.css`.
- **Cross-løypa**: the slope, new for 2026/27, that links hovedløypa and barnebakken. It ends at the top of barnebakken. Its GPS track is `docs/crossloype.kml`, which has two ~41 m points from the road tunnel under the slope. Filter those out before use.
